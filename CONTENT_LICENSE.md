Original source URLs, byte sizes, SHA-256 checksums, retrieval dates, attribution and policy links are recorded in [government-sources.json](packages/content/government-sources.json) and included in the source manifest. Their bytes are preserved; official banners are displayed with their full proportions. Government preview images can be cached offline, while linked programme websites remain online resources.

The four colourful lesson illustrations under `apps/pwa/public/learning/` are original Smriva vector artwork, available under CC BY 4.0 with the attribution above. They accompany Smriva's original activities and do not claim government authorship.

Other linked websites, videos, documents and textbooks are external references and are not redistributed. Application dependencies retain their own licenses.
